The Investment Thesis: Own the Deterministic Control Plane for Healthcare AI

Every hospital, clinic group, and health system in Europe and North America is under simultaneous pressure to adopt clinical AI (ambient scribing, triage, coding) while complying with EU NIS2, the EU AI Act, and GDPR Article 9. Point-solution AI wrappers are stalling in procurement because they cannot prove deterministic clinical safety or API authorization security.

SlainteTech AI Security Labs provides the missing infrastructure layer: a sovereign HL7 FHIR R4 + Model Context Protocol (FastMCP) control plane where autonomous security, governance, clinical, and revenue agents operate under mathematical guardrails.
