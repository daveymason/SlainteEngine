Who This Is Built For

Primary Decision-Makers: Hospital Chief Pharmacists, Community Pharmacy Chain Directors & Superintendent Pharmacists.

The Industry Problem in Clinical & Community Pharmacies

  • Community and hospital dispensaries often screen interactions without visibility into the patient's latest eGFR or serum potassium.
  • Alert fatigue from noisy legacy databases causes high-severity interactions (Clarithromycin + Statin, Warfarin + Metronidazole) to be clicked through.
  • Manual reconciliation of Irish GMS numbers (`urn:oid:1.3.6.1.4.1.54392.5.2690.42.80`) and dispensing fees creates end-of-month HSE PCRS claim rejections.

End-to-End Clinical Workflow

  1. Open the SlaintePharm Live Dispensing Queue showing all active MedicationRequests enriched with Rx-Guard safety status.
