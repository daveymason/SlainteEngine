Who This Is Built For

Primary Decision-Makers: Chief Psychiatrists, Private Mental Health Hospital Directors, CAMHS / Community Mental Health Leads & DPOs.

The Industry Problem in Psychiatrists & Mental Health

  • 60–90 minute initial psychiatric assessments and Mental Health Act tribunals generate heavy narrative documentation burdens.
  • Complex psychotropic polypharmacy hazards: Lithium toxicity when eGFR declines or NSAIDs/ACEi are added, CNS/respiratory depression with benzodiazepine-opioid co-prescribing, and CYP450 interactions.
  • Psychiatric notes require strict separation from general hospital staff — unauthorized snooping is a catastrophic GDPR Art. 9 & HIQA breach.
