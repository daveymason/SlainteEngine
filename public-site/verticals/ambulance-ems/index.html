The Industry Problem in Ambulance & Paramedic Services

  • Radio voice handovers lose critical vital sign trends and medication timestamps before the ambulance reaches the ED bay.
  • Cluttered tablet ePCR forms take 15+ minutes to complete on scene instead of focusing on immediate resuscitation.
  • Paper casualty cards at mass-casualty or inter-facility transfers require manual re-entry on arrival.

End-to-End Clinical Workflow

  1. Enter field vitals and chief complaint in the high-contrast Rapid Field ePCR console (or snap a photo of casualty notes).
  2. FastMCP computes deterministic MTS Category (e.g. Cat 1 Immediate — 0 min target) and NEWS2 risk tier.
  3. Click 'Transmit ED Pre-Alert' to index the HL7 FHIR R4 Bundle directly into the receiving hospital's Live Triage Queue.
