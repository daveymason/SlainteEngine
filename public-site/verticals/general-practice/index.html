Who This Is Built For

Primary Decision-Makers: GP Managing Partners, Primary Care Network Directors & Chronic Disease Management (CDM) Leads.

The Industry Problem in General Practitioners (GPs)

  • GPs spending 90–120 minutes after clinic typing SOAP notes, hospital referral letters, and coding HSE Chronic Disease Management reviews.
  • Hospital discharge summaries and lab PDFs arriving in messy formats that bury critical eGFR drops or medication changes.
  • Cloud AI scribes transmitting identifiable patient conversations outside the EU without GDPR Art. 9 data-minimisation controls.

End-to-End Clinical Workflow

  1. Open the SlainteGP CDM roster showing HbA1c, clinic BP, eGFR targets, and Irish GMS eligibility.
