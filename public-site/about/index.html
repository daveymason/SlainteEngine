Mission & Engineering Philosophy

Making Autonomous Clinical AI Provably Safe, Auditable & Sovereign

Born from Irish healthcare interoperability and offensive API security research, SlainteTech AI Security Labs was founded on a simple rule: no language model should ever guess a vital-sign deterioration score, invent a drug interaction result, or silently leak special-category patient records.

Our Three Non-Negotiable Engineering Rules

Deterministic Guardrails Over Probabilistic Hope

Every physiological score (RCP NEWS2, Manchester Triage) and pharmacology check (Allergy cross-reactivity, ISMP DDI, LOINC eGFR) is executed by deterministic Python FastMCP code covered by automated regression tests.

Continuous Offensive Security Inside the Clinic

Healthcare APIs are prime ransomware and BOLA targets. Our SlainteShield squad continuously verifies patient compartment isolation, dependency pins, and Subresource Integrity hashes.

Built for Real Frontline Conditions

Whether used by an Emergency Consultant at a workstation, a Care-at-Home Nurse on a phone, a Paramedic on a rugged tablet, or a Crew Medical Officer in an offline habitat, the UI stays fast, accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA), and calm.
